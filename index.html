About

The in-between is where I work

Growing up in Guatemala during and after the peace process, I saw the long and often invisible work of reconstruction that follows conflict. Peace, I learned, comes from changing the structures and relationships underneath a conflict, slowly and over time.

I bring that lens to my work across philanthropy, multilateral institutions, and nonprofits. I work where strategy meets execution. I help leaders see where things aren't connecting, frame the choices in front of them, and build the systems that let teams move fast without cutting corners. I treat monitoring, evaluation, and learning as a way to understand what is actually happening, and I adjust as conditions change.

In nearly a decade of work, I've moved from coordinating projects to building the systems behind them, and now to shaping special initiatives alongside senior leadership. I've helped stand up a sister organization, coordinated a 20-country poll, and built the grantmaking, finance, and governance systems for a bi-national nonprofit from scratch. I'm at my best on work that doesn't belong to any one team, where I can get people aligned and turn something complex into a plan a board, a funder, or a partner can act on.
